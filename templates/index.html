How a story moves through the desk

  1. In: raw items arrive and get grouped automatically when their wording overlaps enough to likely be the same event.
  2. Reporter: reviews the grouped sources, writes or edits the brief, and can fold in an item the grouping missed — then submits it.
  3. Editor: rewrites as needed and publishes. A reporter account can never do this step.
  4. Published: the brief is locked. If a duplicate turns up later, it gets linked to the published story as a source — the published text and time never change.

This prototype simulates roles with the switcher in the top right instead of real logins, but every publish and merge action is still checked server-side against the acting role.
